Purpose of SBA Grant Programs

Established by the U.S. Congress, the Small Business Administration exists to aid, counsel, assist, and protect the interests of American small businesses. Grant initiatives support:

  • Small business participation in federally funded projects
  • Research, innovation, and technology advancement
  • Community development and economic stabilization
  • Accountability and transparency in public funding
